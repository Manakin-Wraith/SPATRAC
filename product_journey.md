# Product Journey: Detailed Workflow for SPATRAC Traceability System

## **1️⃣ Receiving**

### **Trigger:**  
Supplier delivers a product.

### **Actions:**  
1. **Log Supplier Details:**  
   - Record the supplier's name, contact information, and certifications.  
   - Verify compliance with food safety standards (e.g., HACCP, ISO certifications).  
2. **Batch Number & Expiry Date:**  
   - Document the batch number and expiration date for the incoming product.  
   - Ensure traceability for potential recalls or audits.  
3. **Assign Unique Product Identifier (UPID):**  
   - Generate a unique ID for each product batch (QR code or barcode).  
   - Ensure the UPID can be scanned and tracked throughout the lifecycle.  
4. **Inspection & Quality Check:**  
   - Record any deviations, damages, or issues (e.g., damaged packaging or incorrect temperature during transport).  
   - Flag non-compliant batches for quarantine or rejection.  

### **Food Security Benefits:**  
- Establishes the first record for product traceability.  
- Enables quick rejection or quarantine of compromised deliveries.  

---

## **2️⃣ Storage**

### **Trigger:**  
Product is moved to its designated storage area.

### **Actions:**  
1. **Log Storage Location:**  
   - Assign a specific storage unit (e.g., fridge, freezer, shelf).  
   - Record the exact location to streamline retrieval and minimize cross-contamination risks.  
2. **Update Stock Levels:**  
   - Adjust inventory levels to reflect the newly received product.  
   - Maintain real-time visibility into stock availability.  
3. **Condition Monitoring:**  
   - Track storage conditions such as temperature, humidity, and expiration dates.  
   - Set up alerts for deviations (e.g., a fridge exceeding safe temperature thresholds).  

### **Food Security Benefits:**  
- Ensures compliance with food storage regulations.  
- Reduces spoilage and cross-contamination risks.  

---

## **3️⃣ Department Collection**

### **Trigger:**  
A department (e.g., Butchery, Bakery, HMR) requests a product.

### **Actions:**  
1. **Log Transfer Details:**  
   - Record the product identifier, department name, requested quantity, and timestamp.  
   - Document the person responsible for handling the transfer for accountability.  
2. **Assign Department Identifier:**  
   - Link the product batch to the requesting department.  
   - Track usage for each department.  
3. **Flag Low Stock:**  
   - Notify storage managers if inventory levels fall below a predefined threshold.  

### **Food Security Benefits:**  
- Tracks product movement, creating accountability.  
- Ensures only required quantities are moved, reducing waste.  

---

## **4️⃣ Processing**

### **Trigger:**  
The product is used as an ingredient in a recipe.

### **Actions:**  
1. **Link to Final Product:**  
   - Record the use of ingredient batches in creating a final product.  
   - Document batch numbers, quantities, and timestamps of use.  
2. **Generate Traceability Record:**  
   - Map each ingredient batch to the corresponding final product batch.  
   - Maintain a detailed traceability chain for backward and forward tracking.  
3. **Recipe Auditing:**  
   - Log recipe details, including allergen and nutritional information.  
   - Ensure compliance with food safety standards during preparation.  

### **Food Security Benefits:**  
- Enables backward traceability: Identify which ingredients were used in a product.  
- Enables forward traceability: Identify which products contain a flagged ingredient.  

---

## **5️⃣ Distribution**

### **Trigger:**  
Final product is ready for storage, sale, or distribution.

### **Actions:**  
1. **Log Final Product Batch Details:**  
   - Assign a new product ID for the final product.  
   - Link it to the ingredient batches used during processing.  
2. **Storage/Distribution Records:**  
   - Log the location of the final product (e.g., fridge, shelf, or shipping).  
   - Track shipments to retailers, customers, or other facilities.  
3. **Flag Recall Alerts:**  
   - Implement a system to flag and recall specific batches if safety concerns arise.  
   - Notify stakeholders (e.g., customers, distributors) about the recall.  

### **Food Security Benefits:**  
- Maintains an end-to-end traceability record from supplier to customer.  
- Facilitates targeted recalls, minimizing health risks and financial losses.

# **Additional Features to Enhance the Journey**

### **Audit Trail for Compliance**  
- Record all activities with timestamps for regulatory compliance (e.g., HACCP, ISO).  
- Provide detailed reports for food safety audits.  

### **Alerts & Notifications**  
- Real-time alerts for:  
  - Expired products.  
  - Storage condition deviations.  
  - Stock shortages.  
- Notifications for flagged products to facilitate recalls.  

### **Recall Workflow**  
1. **Search:** Identify affected product batches by UPID or batch number.  
2. **Isolate:** Quarantine affected stock in storage or departments.  
3. **Trace:** Use traceability records to identify where the product has been distributed.  
4. **Notify:** Send recall notifications to stakeholders (e.g., distributors, retailers).  
5. **Resolve:** Investigate the root cause and document corrective actions.
