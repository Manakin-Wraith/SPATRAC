Based on the provided SPAR audit checklist and supporting documents, a traceability application for a SPAR retailer should track the following key information:

**Product Information:**

* **Product Name:**  The specific name of the product (e.g., Beef Burger Catering - Smart Chef).
* **Final Product Batch Code:** A unique code identifying a specific batch of the final product.  Crucial for targeted recalls.
* **Production/Preparation Date:** The date the product was made or prepared in-store.  Essential for shelf-life management.
* **Sell-By Date/Date of Minimum Durability:** The last date the product should be offered for sale.  Required by law for most products.
* **Use-By Date (where applicable):** For highly perishable items.
* **Quantity Produced/Prepared:**  The amount of product made in a given batch.  Helps with stock reconciliation during a recall.
* **Ingredients List:** A complete list of all ingredients in the product, including additives and allergens.  This should follow the descending order of mass as required by R146, with allergens clearly highlighted (e.g., "Allergens: Contains: Wheat (gluten), soya, egg, milk").
* **Known Value Items (KVIs):**  Identify and track KVIs used in production, particularly for high-risk items.
* **Previously Frozen Status:** If a product was previously frozen, this must be clearly indicated on the label and in your records.  Important for fish, some meats, etc.

**Supplier Information (for ingredients and packaging):**

* **Supplier Name:** The name of the company supplying the ingredient or packaging material.
* **Supplier Address and Contact Details:** Full address, phone number, and contact person.  Needed to quickly contact suppliers during a recall.
* **Invoice/Delivery Note Number:** Links the product to a specific delivery.
* **Product/Ingredient Batch Code:** The supplier's batch code for the ingredient or packaging. Enables tracing back to the source.
* **Receiving Date:** The date the ingredient or packaging was received by the store.  Helps manage FIFO.
* **Country of Origin:** Required on labels and for traceability.
* **Direct Supplier Agreement & Food Safety Certificate (for direct suppliers):**  Keep records of these documents for all direct suppliers who don't supply through the SPAR DC.  Essential for due diligence.

**Packaging Information:**

* **Packaging Type:**  Description of the packaging (e.g., small foil container).
* **Packaging Batch Code:**  Unique code for the batch of packaging material.
* **Packaging Traceability:** Track the quantity of packaging received, used, and remaining, along with batch codes, as shown in the "CHECKLIST_Packaging_Traceability" example.

**In-Store Processes and Personnel:**

* **Food Handler Name:** The name of the person responsible for preparing the product.  Vital for investigating potential contamination sources.
* **Department/Area:** The specific department or area of the store where the product was prepared (e.g., HMR Hot Kitchen).
* **Equipment Used:** List the equipment used in making the product.  Helps with cleaning and sanitation tracking if contamination is suspected.
* **Storage Location (including temperature):** Track where products are stored, along with temperature logs for fridges, freezers, and hot holding units.
* **Temperature Monitoring:**  Regularly record product and equipment temperatures, as per R638. Your application should ideally integrate with temperature sensors for automated recording and alerts. Include core/surface temperature distinction.
* **Cleaning and Sanitization Records:**  Record cleaning schedules, responsible staff, and verification by supervisors, as per the "CHECKLIST_Master_Cleaning_Fresh_Departments" example. Link these records to the equipment used for specific products.
* **Pest Control Records:**  Maintain detailed records of pest control activities, including service reports, bait station maps, and corrective actions.

**Report Output for Auditing:**

The report output should be designed to meet the requirements of the SPAR audit and regulatory requirements (R638, R146). It should be easily accessible, printable, and exportable (e.g., to PDF or Excel).

The report should include the following sections:

* **Product Summary:** Product name, batch code, production date, sell-by/use-by date, quantity produced, previously frozen status.
* **Ingredient Traceability:**  For each ingredient: Name, supplier name and details, batch code, receiving date, country of origin.
* **Packaging Traceability:** Packaging type, supplier name and details, batch code, date received, quantity received, stock issued information.
* **Production Information:** Food handler name, department/area, equipment used, storage location and temperature.
* **Temperature Records:** A table or graph showing temperature readings over time, with clear indications of any out-of-range temperatures and corrective actions taken.
* **Cleaning and Sanitization Records:**  Cleaning schedules, chemicals used, responsible staff, and supervisor verification.
* **Pest Control Records:**  Pest control service reports, bait station maps, trend reports, and corrective actions.
* **Recall Information (if applicable):** Details of any recalls, including products affected, actions taken, and parties notified.

This structure will ensure that you have all the information readily available for audits, enabling you to demonstrate compliance with SPAR standards and legal requirements.  The use of electronic records, if managed correctly, is typically acceptable to auditors, but always best to confirm with your specific auditor.
